I think I have lost creativity. And what’s worse? I have become a full time consumer, only a consumer. 
I was reminded recently, while watching the trailer of some show’s episode, that how we used to say, ‘I’m bored’ as kids. Come to think of it, I am never bored now. Every time the tiniest speck of boredom touches me, I have my phone to the rescue. Endless scrolls. Enough content to spend my lifetime with. And when there’s so much to consume, how will I create?
Boredom held the key to creativity as a kid. I drew or painted or wrote or spoke to a friend or went out for a walk. I did something. My brain produced something. Now, it’s just a flat mush with information flowing all over it, and ironically, nothing registers. I blame this for all my woes - decline in general intelligence, decrease in physical activity, lack of something that really interests me. The bar for novelty and activity is so high to my beautiful brain, that normal life seems terribly boring. Of course I am sadder and lazier than before.

Maybe it’s time I promise myself to create. Just something, one thing, every week. Something that gets my brain in the habit of creating, thinking, and failing. This consumption cushion needs to go. I was born to create, connect and conclude. Go Pratham, do something cool!
